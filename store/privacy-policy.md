# QBR — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-26_

QBR ("Quarterly Business Reports") is a single-player card game. **It does not collect,
transmit, sell or share any personal data.**

## What the app stores, and where

Everything QBR remembers stays **on your device**, in the app's own local storage:

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Your record (years and careers played, wins, best rung, stakes cleared, daily-career streak) | To show your progress and unlock special cards |
| Your deck choices (which special cards you have benched) | So your deck stays the way you left it |
| Your settings (sound, haptics) | So the app respects them next time |
| Which tips you have already seen | So a tip is shown only once |

None of this ever leaves your device. It is not linked to your identity, and we cannot see
it. Deleting the app deletes it; **Settings → Reset progress** in the app erases your
record and unlocks at any time.

## What the app does not do

- No account, sign-in, or registration.
- No network requests of any kind: no servers, no analytics, no advertising, no crash
  reporting, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
- No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera, microphone or other apps.

The **daily career** is the same for every player because it is computed from the date on
your device — not downloaded from a server.

## Children

QBR does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a future version ever changes any of the above (for example, a nearby two-player mode),
this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the App Store privacy details
will be updated with it.
